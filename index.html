Everything you need to know

Do I need any experience to start dropshipping with DropPilot?

Not at all. DropPilot is built for beginners and pros alike. Our guided setup, daily product picks and automation handle the technical bits, so you can focus on choosing products and running ads.

Are the suppliers really shipping from the UK?

Yes. Our network includes vetted suppliers with UK and EU warehouses offering 1–3 day delivery. You can also filter to UK-only fulfilment to keep delivery times fast and refunds low.

Does it handle VAT and £GBP correctly?

Absolutely. All analytics are in £GBP, and we generate UK VAT-compliant invoices automatically. Profit reports deduct VAT, fees, COGS and ad spend so you see your true net profit.

Which platforms does DropPilot integrate with?

Shopify, WooCommerce, TikTok Shop, Amazon UK and eBay for selling; Meta & TikTok for ads; Royal Mail & Evri for tracking; plus Klaviyo and Stripe. New integrations ship monthly.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes — no contracts, no lock-in. Cancel in one click from your dashboard. Your 14-day trial requires no card, so there's zero risk in trying it.
