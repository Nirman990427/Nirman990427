FAQ

Questions, answered.

Do I need to connect a bank account to start?

No. You can create and send invoices on the free plan straight away. Connecting your own Stripe or GoCardless account is optional — it just lets clients pay you by card or direct debit. FlowBooks never holds your money; payouts go directly to your account.

Can I switch from spreadsheets or another tool?

Yes. Import your clients and historical invoices from a CSV in a few clicks. If you get stuck, our support team will help you move across — no migration fee.

How do automatic reminders work?

You choose the schedule — for example, a friendly nudge two days before the due date and a follow-up if an invoice goes overdue. Reminders are sent on your behalf, with your branding, and you can pause them for any client at any time.

Is my data safe, and can I leave?

Your data is encrypted in transit and at rest, protected by two-factor sign-in. You own it: export everything to CSV or PDF whenever you like, and delete your account in one click if you ever decide to go.

What does it cost after the trial?

Nothing changes automatically. When your 14-day Pro trial ends you simply drop back to the free Starter plan unless you choose to upgrade. We'll never charge a card you didn't enter.
